# Against

The evidence against the repository's rules and the controversies. Nothing is dropped to make a rule look stronger. Terms are in [frame](frame.md).

## The cost of late defects

- Boehm 2001 says a late fix is often 100 times dearer, and gives 5:1 for small noncritical systems. The word "often" was added in the updated list.
- Menzies 2017 analysed 171 projects and found no evidence for the delayed-issue effect (secondary).
- It suggests the effect is not constant across projects.
- Consequence: the repository must not claim that early work is cheaper by a fixed ratio.

## Success figures

- Eveleens 2010 applied the Chaos report's definitions to 5,457 forecasts of 1,211 projects and found the Chaos figures did not reflect the data (secondary).
- Consequence: a failure rate from that report is not used here.

## Teaching heuristics

- Hembree 1992, in secondary summaries: heuristics had a clear effect only with trained teachers, and fixed steps scored below flexible approaches.
- Consequence: a fixed list of steps is a convention, not a measured aid.

## A taught personal process

- Prechelt 2001b found the improvements from PSP training smaller than proponents assume, possibly from low actual use.
- Consequence: a method written down is not a method followed.

## Avoidable rework is not all bad

- Fairley 2005: iteration needs some rework, and too little may mean too little review.
- Consequence: the aim is fewer wasted round trips, not none.

## Weak measurement of frameworks

- Serrador 2015 is correlational. The guides of Schwaber 2020, Singer 2019 and Chapman 2000 cite no measurement in the parts read.
- Ubl 2020 is the owner's claim about its own practice and scores below the threshold.

## What a skeptic could say of this review

- Sixteen formal works are located, not opened.
- One reviewer scored the grey sources; no second rater.
- The searches were bounded at 10 results per string.
