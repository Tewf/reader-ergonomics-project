# 0002: Keep the name, name the baseline

**Decision:** The review found no single field name for the problem, so the repository keeps its provisional name, and B1 is the baseline the maintainer's practice is compared with.

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-02. **Shape:** [decision record](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/blob/2e5b6f0c3e67d2dc8195a63cf882d46170e79a5a/shapes/decision-record.md).

## Context

- Record 0001 left the name provisional until the review's frame step.
- The frame step found parts, not a whole: the design science research process for the steps, evidence-based research for the review, project organization for the trail ([frame](../evidence/project-method/frame.md)).
- Two guides on the trail, Noble 2009 and Wilson 2017, write "project" for one person's work, as this repository does.
- The review ends only when it names a baseline ([baseline](../evidence/project-method/baseline.md)).

## Decision

- The repository keeps the name `reader-ergonomics-project`.
- B1 is the baseline: the six activities of Peffers 2006 for the steps, and the project organization of Noble 2009 and Wilson 2017 for the trail.
- B1 is a named comparison, not a result. It moves when a better-evidenced model or a measured study replaces it.

## Consequences

- No address changes, and the neighbours' links stay valid.
- A reader who searches for the design science process will not find it in the name. The front page and the frame carry that term.
- **Bad:** a name that fits one part of the field only, and a baseline taken from a synthesis rather than a test.
