# Problem

The problem of the first job, in one sentence, and the names the field gives it.

**Shape:** [topic](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics-topic/blob/86e14fe9a1a9877d6a87a2f8b4e8713f98724b5a/shapes/topic.md)

**Problem:** How does one person go from a stated problem to a checked result with the fewest wasted round trips, and leave a trail another person can resume?

## The field's names

- No single field owns the problem. The steps are the design science research process in research and the project phases in practice.
- The review before building is evidence-based research. The trail is project organization.
- The repository's word **project** matches Noble 2009 and Wilson 2017. The name stays (decision 0002).
